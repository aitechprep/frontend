# AITechPrep product requirements

## Product intent

Help AI and software engineering candidates understand technical concepts and explain them clearly in interviews. The learning experience prioritizes comprehension over maximizing content volume.

## Current MVP

- Six technical tracks and exactly 12 interview questions, two per track.
- Question pages with a concise answer, fundamentals, accessible visual explanation, practical examples, technical detail, trade-offs, takeaways, and answered follow-ups.
- One seven-stage AI Engineer Foundations roadmap with topic pages.
- Blog & News listing and article pages with sourced, dated industry news.
- Cross-content search and anonymous saved questions stored in the current browser.
- Responsive navigation, page-specific metadata, and static pre-rendering.

## Intended audience

People preparing for AI, machine-learning, Python, data-engineering, MLOps, and system-design interviews, including learners who benefit from clear plain-language explanations.

## Non-goals of the current MVP

There are no accounts, server-side bookmark synchronization, paid features, learning analytics dashboard, content administration system, or commitment to a specific interview outcome. Do not add these without an explicit product decision.

## Quality requirements

Content must be accurate, legible, appropriately sourced, and explicit about uncertainty or limitations. The site should work with keyboard and touch, reflow on mobile, preserve browser-local save feedback, and avoid unsupported claims in editorial or marketing content.

## Open launch requirements

Production domain configuration, hosting-specific 404 behavior, legal policies and operating-entity details, real contact channels, analytics privacy/consent review, and any lead-capture or subscription flows require owner input before public launch.
